# South Savory — refined logo kit

The linked SS, two orange dots, serif name and 2023 founding year carry the original identity forward. The long rules and ornamental curls have been removed for simpler layouts.

## Use

- Use the full name when introducing the brand; use the SS symbol when the name is already clear.
- Place the dark logos on white or a light neutral background. The dark banner includes its background and is not a transparent overlay.
- Preserve proportions. Leave at least one orange-dot diameter of empty space around the visible logo.
- Check readability at the actual size. If ESTD / 2023 becomes too small, switch to the horizontal version or monogram.
- For a round social profile, scale the monogram down until the full symbol fits inside the circular crop.

## Format and production

These are AI-generated raster logo refinements, with small variations between layouts. The three transparent files have genuine alpha transparency. They can be placed in digital layouts and used for brand direction. They are not editable vector masters or certified print-ready artwork. Before large signage, embroidery, stamps or final packaging production, redraw the selected logo as a consistent SVG / outlined PDF and proof it with the supplier.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The final prompts are in generation-prompts.json.
